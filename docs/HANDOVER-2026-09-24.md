## Gotchas discovered this session
- Out-of-frame samples near the top of frame: tracer uses screen-space clipping, tangent lead-in to y=h-8; big driver apexes intentionally exit the top of frame and re-enter (matches approved browser mockup).
- `Dp * Int` is fine, `Int * Dp` is NOT (compile error) — use `dp * 2` not `2 * dp`.
- Compose: Box needed for vertical centering inside fixed-height rail buttons (Text wraps, Box centers).
- BLE app-side: MissingPermission lint requires @SuppressLint at call sites (disconnectClient, onConnectRequested).
